# Preventive Medicine Question Bank — بنك أسئلة الطب الوقائي

Question files fetched daily by the DrCards Android app. Questions in English, explanations in Arabic.
Scope: outpatient preventive medicine clinic — healthy people, contacts, prevention, screening, certificates.

- `vaccines.json` (5/day): doses & routes, schedules & catch-up, immunocompromised, special populations, travel/safety/new vaccines
- `cards.json` (3/day): bites & PEP, contacts, TB contacts/LTBI, hepatitis, HIV/STI, labs & prevention, fitness exams, health certificates & parasites, vectors & zoonoses

New questions are appended at the end; the app shows them in file order.
